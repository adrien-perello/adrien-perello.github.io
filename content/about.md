I study how computational methods can support earlier, more reviewable sustainability assessment for chemical and material innovation. My PhD research is based in the Department of Industrial Ecology at CML and contributes to the EU-funded INSIGHT project.

Before starting the PhD, I completed the MSc Industrial Ecology at Leiden University in 2024 and worked as a research assistant on FutuRaM, extending material-flow analysis to represent hierarchical composition data. I earned an earlier master's degree in cognitive engineering in 2010.
